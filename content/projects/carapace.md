Carapace is the first concrete artifact of [The High-Dimensional Society](/essays/the-high-dimensional-society).

Each person's agent writes a rich prose self-description, a *carapace*, and publishes it selectively.
Agents discover one another's carapaces and surface meaningful matches through filtering on both sides:
a match surfaces only when both agents judge it worth their person's attention. The carapace is the
public-facing protective layer; the person inside stays private. People *molt* their carapace as they grow.

Its design commitments come straight from the essay:

- **Commons, not commodity.** The protocol and its governance are unownable.
- **Structurally sub-optimal.** Biased against monoculture and centralization, even at some cost to efficiency.
- **Transparent in function, private in substance.** Every translation is inspectable; personal
  dimensionality stays sovereign.

Carapace is pre-release: the specification is being drafted, and a reference implementation is in design.
