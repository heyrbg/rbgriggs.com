*The High-Dimensional Society* is a speculative novel, being written during
[PiBoWriMo 2026](https://protocolized.summerofprotocols.com/p/announcing-pibowrimo-2026), the Protocol
Institute's book-writing month, starting November 2026.

It imagines the world argued for in the essay
[The High-Dimensional Society](/essays/the-high-dimensional-society) (January 2026). Modern society scales by
compressing people into simple proxies, then overfits on them until whatever the proxies can't see withers:
what the essay calls *dimensional poverty*. AI, for the first time in human history, offers the possibility of
protocols that coordinate human societies at a planetary scale without flattening human complexity into the
conformity that modern-day proxies require.

Progress will be posted here and in the [notes](/notes) as the writing develops.
