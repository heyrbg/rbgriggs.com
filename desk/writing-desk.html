    How this desk works. Notes live as markdown files in the site's GitHub repo. Writing and publishing happen in your conversation with Claude, which updates this desk after each change.

    Buttons here send requests to Claude: draft an idea, revise a note, save your own edits, change details, publish. Each request shows as Waiting for Claude until it has been applied to the site, then the desk updates.

    You can also comment on any note, or select a passage and comment on just that, and choose Send to Claude.
